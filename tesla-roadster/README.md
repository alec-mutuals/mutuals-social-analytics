# Tesla Roadster collection report

A static, public report of a saved 40-post TikTok search collected on 16 October 2026. Open `index.html` through a local HTTP server; no build, credentials, tracking or backend required.

`data/posts.csv` preserves public post fields as collected. `data/posts.json` is a normalized export with collection metadata, public post fields, video duration and local preview paths. Internal actor receipts and signed CDN URLs are not included. `data.js` supplies the same data to the UI without an extra request.

Collection: queries `tesla roadster` and `tesla roadster backflip`; past 24 hours; most liked; 20 results per query; clockworks/tiktok-scraper 0.0.619. One snapshot; no growth estimates or unique reach claims.

Images are attributed to their original creators through the adjacent source links. The full collection screenshot is included as requested. This report is not affiliated with Tesla or TikTok.

## Selected visual reviews

The five largest posts by views plus the highest share/view-ratio post have visual analyses in `data/analyses.json` and `analyses.js`. Click a highlighted card in the overview image or a selected post preview to open the native analysis dialog. `#post-<id>` opens a review directly. The six-post filter limits the browse grid to reviewed posts.

Each review was produced from actual local source videos: import metadata with Social Trends `references import`, prepare with `references prepare`, inspect timestamped frames, and persist the evidence with `references analyze`. A total of 101 frames were inspected across the six clips; the public page includes representative frames for each scene group. Reviews are visual-only: no continuous playback, audio, precise cut detection, or seamless loop verification. No Jev call was made. Analysis IDs and media hashes let the public descriptions be traced to the local records; local paths and footage are not published.

Scene ranges group observed samples rather than claiming exact editing boundaries. Ideas to test are editorial hypotheses, separate from observations and engagement metrics.
