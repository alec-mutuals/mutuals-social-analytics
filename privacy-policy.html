Privacy Policy — Mutuals Social Analytics

Last updated: 22 June 2026

Mutuals Social Analytics (the "Tool") is an internal application operated by Mutuals Inc ("we", "us") to retrieve and analyze performance metrics for social media accounts that we own and operate. This policy explains what data the Tool accesses and how it is handled.

Who this applies to

The Tool is used only by Mutuals Inc and the authorized operators of the social media accounts it connects to. It is not a public-facing product and does not serve third-party end users.

Data we access

  • Profile information and content metadata (e.g. video/post IDs, captions, permalinks, publish dates) for accounts that explicitly authorize the Tool.
  • Engagement metrics provided by each platform's official API (e.g. views, likes, comments, shares, reach).
  • OAuth access tokens issued by the platform to authorize these read requests.

We access this data exclusively through the official APIs of the respective platforms (e.g. TikTok, Meta/Instagram, YouTube), only for accounts that grant authorization, and only with the scopes required to read analytics.

How we use it

Data is used solely to display and analyze the performance of our own content internally. We do not sell it, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising or profiling of any individuals.

Storage and retention

Retrieved metrics and access tokens are stored securely in our private systems and retained only as long as needed for internal analysis. Access tokens can be revoked at any time from the connected account's settings, which immediately stops the Tool's access.

Deletion

To request deletion of stored data, or to revoke access, contact us at the address below. Revoking the app's authorization on the platform also terminates all further data access.
