Mutuals Social Analytics

An internal analytics tool operated by Mutuals Inc that retrieves performance metrics for the social media accounts we own and operate, using the official APIs of each platform (TikTok, Meta/Instagram, YouTube).

What it does

  • Reads our own accounts' post metrics — views, likes, comments, shares — through each platform's official API, for accounts that authorize the app.
  • Aggregates that data into internal dashboards so we can understand how our content performs across platforms.

What it does not do

  • It is not a public product and has no third-party end users.
  • It does not post, message, scrape, or access data without authorization.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.
